## 🧠 How I Approach Data Problems

I try to keep the analytical process grounded in the problem rather than the tool.

**1. Understand the problem**

What decision needs to be supported?

**2. Understand the data**

What does the data actually measure? Is it complete, consistent, and suitable for the question?

**3. Analyse**

Apply statistical, analytical, or machine-learning methods appropriate to the problem.

**4. Communicate**

Translate the results into findings that are understandable and useful to the intended audience.

**5. Question the result**

Consider limitations, assumptions, uncertainty, and what additional evidence would be needed before making stronger conclusions.
